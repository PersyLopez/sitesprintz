## 🔄 Phase 1 Status: CORE BUSINESS MODE SYSTEM ✅ COMPLETE

The critical architectural problem has been solved. All templates now:
- ✅ Auto-detect solo vs. team operations
- ✅ Dynamically show/hide provider selection
- ✅ Adapt booking flow based on business size
- ✅ Support intelligent staff assignment ("Any Available" with load balancing)
- ✅ Enable service-specific staff assignments

## 📋 Phase 2: Niche-Specific Enhancements (Research & Planning)

This document presents research on *additional* features that could enhance each template beyond the core booking system. These are enhancement opportunities, not committed features.

### Important Note
The items below are **aspirational improvements** identified through industry research. They represent opportunities for deeper customization per niche. Implementation priority and timeline are to be determined through product roadmap planning.

## 🏠 Template-by-Template Analysis

### 1. 💇 SALON & SPA TEMPLATE

**Phase 1 Status:** ✅ Core business mode system integrated  
**Phase 2 Research:** Salon-specific enhancement opportunities below

**What Phase 1 Provided:**
- ✅ Solo salon support (no stylist selection when only 1 person)
- ✅ Team salon support (stylist selection for multi-person operations)
- ✅ "Any Available Stylist" option for flexible clients
- ✅ Service-specific stylist assignment

#### 🔍 Phase 2 Enhancement Opportunities

**Client Experience:**
- Client preference saving - Remember each client's preferred stylist
- Service add-ons - Deep conditioning, scalp treatment, nail art selections
- Consultation booking - Free consultations for complex services like color correction
- Before/after portfolio - Per-stylist galleries to build individual brand

**Revenue & Retention:**
- Loyalty points system - Track points across visits
- Referral rewards - "Refer a friend" discount incentives
- Product recommendations - Aftercare products linked to services

**Operational:**
- Same-stylist guarantee badge - "Always see your preferred stylist" promise
- Price range display - "Starting at $X" for variable-length services
- Chair rental mode - For salon owners renting chairs to independent stylists

#### 💡 Research Insights
Salon businesses value:
- **High Impact:** Client loyalty and consistency (stylist preference saves time)
- **Revenue Driver:** Add-on services and product recommendations
- **Market Segment:** Chair rental is growing segment worth supporting

### 2. 🍽️ RESTAURANT TEMPLATE

**Phase 1 Status:** ✅ Core business mode system integrated  
**Phase 2 Research:** Restaurant-specific enhancement opportunities below

**What Phase 1 Provided:**
- ✅ Solo/small restaurant support (simple booking flow)
- ✅ Team support (private dining coordinators, event handling)
- ✅ Reservation management
- ✅ Capacity-aware bookings

#### 🔍 Phase 2 Enhancement Opportunities

**Operational Efficiency:**
- Waitlist system - "Join waitlist" when fully booked with wait time estimates
- QR code menus - Dynamic, updateable menu without reprinting
- Real-time wait times - Current wait estimate displayed
- Table/room selection - Visual table map for choosing seating preference

**Revenue & Conversion:**
- Online ordering/takeout toggle - Separate flow for to-go orders
- Catering inquiry form - Corporate event requests
- Gift cards - Pre-purchase gift cards for future visits
- Happy hour scheduling - Time-based pricing tiers and promotions

**Guest Experience:**
- Allergen matrix - Detailed allergen information per dish
- Seasonal menu indicator - Clear seasonal item labeling
- Special occasion flags - Anniversary/birthday/celebration notes

#### 💡 Research Insights
Restaurant businesses prioritize:
- **Critical:** Reducing no-shows and managing no-shows
- **Revenue:** Upselling gift cards, catering, and takeout
- **Safety:** Allergen information for liability and guest safety

**Phase 1 Status:** ✅ Core business mode system integrated

**What Phase 1 Provided:**

- Class waitlist - Join waitlist for fully-booked classes

#### 💡 Research Insights
Fitness businesses focus on:
- **Critical:** Member retention through engagement and variety
- **Revenue:** Upselling services (PT, meal plans, add-on classes)
- **Growth:** Referrals through trial classes and guest passes

## 🔄 Universal Improvements (Phase 2 Considerations)

These features could enhance ANY template:

### 1. Business Mode Display
- UI clearly shows whether business is solo/team/hybrid
- Easy mode switching in admin dashboard
- Auto-suggestion when business structure changes

### 2. Communication Preferences
- Customize "no preference" text per template
- Set communication channels (phone, email, SMS, chat)
- Auto-responder templates for different modes

### 3. Trust Indicators
- Mode-appropriate credential display
- Company insurance vs. personal license
- Years in business / customer testimonials
